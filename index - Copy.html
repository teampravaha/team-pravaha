Who We Are

About Team Pravaha

Team Pravaha is a student engineering team from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore, preparing to compete in the Shell Eco-marathon. Founded in October 2025, the team focuses on designing and building ultra-energy-efficient prototype vehicles while fostering innovation, collaboration, and sustainable engineering.

Our multidisciplinary team comprises 18 undergraduate students from various engineering disciplines, working collaboratively across specialised technical subsystems to achieve engineering excellence and develop an ultra-energy-efficient prototype vehicle.

United by a shared passion for innovation, Team Pravaha strives to advance sustainable mobility through practical engineering and continuous learning.

The Challenge

About the Competition

Shell Eco-marathon is one of the world's leading energy-efficiency competitions for student engineering teams. It challenges students to apply innovation, engineering design and real-world problem-solving to develop vehicles that travel as far as possible using the least amount of energy.

The competition brings together more than 200 university teams across regional events in Asia-Pacific, the Americas, and Europe & Africa, creating an international platform for young engineers to test their ideas and demonstrate efficient mobility solutions.

Energy Classes

Battery Electric

Vehicles powered by electrical energy stored in batteries.

Internal Combustion

Vehicles using combustion fuels such as ethanol, gasoline or diesel.

Hydrogen Fuel Cell

Vehicles that use hydrogen fuel cells to generate electrical power.

Competition Categories

Prototype

Designed around maximum energy efficiency, with a streamlined, purpose-built vehicle form.

Urban Concept

Designed to reflect the practicality and everyday usability of road-going vehicles while focusing on energy efficiency.

Our Competition Path

Prototype ICE · E100

Team Pravaha is preparing for the Prototype category with an Internal Combustion Engine running on 100% ethanol (E100).

Ethanol-focused mobility

Our chosen fuel pathway focuses on using pure ethanol as a sustainable alternative fuel.

Flex-fuel development

The project aims to convert a conventional gasoline engine into a flex-fuel ethanol-gasoline engine.
