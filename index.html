Who We Are

About Team Pravaha

Team Pravaha is a student engineering team from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore, preparing to compete in the Shell Eco-marathon. Founded in October 2025, the team focuses on designing and building ultra-energy-efficient prototype vehicles while fostering innovation, collaboration, and sustainable engineering.

Our multidisciplinary team comprises 18 undergraduate students from various engineering disciplines, working collaboratively across specialised technical subsystems to achieve engineering excellence and develop an ultra-energy-efficient prototype vehicle.

United by a shared passion for innovation, Team Pravaha strives to advance sustainable mobility through practical engineering and continuous learning.
